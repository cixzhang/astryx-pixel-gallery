R4 — HTML-in-Canvas drawElementImage (probe summary)

Probed separately (needs --enable-features=CanvasDrawElement in Chromium 153 headless):
• ctx.drawElementImage() exists only with the flag; without it, absent.
• With canvas + child both carrying layoutsubtree, a live DOM subtree rasterises into a 160×120 canvas and displays perfectly pixelated at 3× — real DOM, real low-res raster, no serialisation.
• Programmatic DOM updates + requestPaint() re-render live (message text change verified on screen).
• But canvas children are not hit-testable — a click on the rendered button never reaches it (counter stayed 0); events land on the canvas, so the app must map coordinates itself.
• Readback is blocked (getImageData returns zeros), and the drawn element's size/offset had quirks (element rendered at ~71% of expected width, heading slightly clipped) — an immature implementation.
• Verdict in FINDINGS: the most promising line long-term, not shippable today.
